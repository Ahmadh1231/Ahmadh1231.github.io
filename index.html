Argonne National Laboratory · Quantum Computing Research Intern · June–August 2026

DOE SULI research on superconducting microwave resonators for electron-on-solid-neon devices, including HFSS eigenmode and driven-modal simulation, lithography-ready layouts, and materials characterization. I rebuilt four published resonators from their papers and designed a fifth.

Frequencies landed. Quality factors did not: a paper rarely gives enough detail to rebuild coupling and loss. So I added an audit step. It rejected several of my own early models and retracted some of my Q values. The table says what each model shows and what it does not.

resonatorsimulated vs paperwhat it shows
Parallel-plate7.49999 vs 7.5 GHzFrequency matched. Rebuilt with a coplanar-waveguide feed instead of the paper’s 3D cavity. Layer alignment and loss still open.
Spiral5.987 vs 6 GHzAnalytic, two-level-system and participation-ratio models reproduce the paper. The HFSS model fixes frequency only; its Q is unproven.
Interdigital capacitor7.2198 vs 7.24 GHzFit by construction: the paper never dimensions the capacitor, so I solved finger count and spacing from the target. Coupling cannot be rebuilt from the paper.
Flip-chip spin resonatornot reproducedMy bare-stack model is far from the paper’s 5.55 GHz and the coupling was far too weak. Kept for what it taught me about ports, moats and airbridges.
Fractalnot simulatedAn original design with the physics taken from the literature. Layout only.
